AREESHA GLOBAL WEBSITE

Responsive bilingual website for AL Areesha General Trading LLC.

Main pages: Home, About, Products, Chemicals, Solar Panels & Renewable Energy, Medical Equipment, Blog, Contact.
Arabic pages for all sections are available under /ar/.

Built with HTML5, CSS3, Bootstrap 5 and JavaScript. Header and footer are embedded in each page.

Upload the complete areesha-global-website folder to your web hosting public root. Keep the assets folder and page paths unchanged.

Before launch: connect enquiry forms to a secure backend/email/CRM endpoint. The current form submission is a front-end demo only.

For chemicals, medical devices and solar products, confirm product specifications, applicable certifications, import permissions and destination-market regulatory requirements before listing or supplying specific items.
